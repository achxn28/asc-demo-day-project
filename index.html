A human story

Danielle’s story shows why the data needs a face.

After several family deaths and the loss of a close friend, Danielle experienced months of homelessness and later incarceration. Los Angeles Mission writes that her daughter eventually brought her to the Mission, where Danielle entered a recovery program and began rebuilding toward sobriety, family connection, and work.

The point is not that one story explains the whole crisis. It is that every marker, chart, and count on Bridge LA represents a person whose path is more complicated than a statistic.
