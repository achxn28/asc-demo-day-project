The Story Behind the Numbers

Los Angeles is often seen through its public image: entertainment, tourism, and cultural production. Bridge LA pairs that story with current public data on homelessness and the limits of what those datasets can prove.

Why visibility matters

A large unsheltered share means people and service providers need information that is accurate, current, and explicit about uncertainty. Bridge LA avoids fake availability claims and points users to provider contact information where real-time data is not public.

What to look for in the numbers

Scale

Use the total count to understand the size of the crisis, then avoid treating that number as a complete description of people’s lives.

Exposure

The unsheltered share points to people living without stable indoor protection, which changes urgency, outreach needs, and health risk.

Limits

Counts are estimates at a point in time. Bridge LA should always show source, year, and what the metric does not measure.
