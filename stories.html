Featured story

Danielle’s story gives the site its center.

Los Angeles Mission shares that Danielle experienced major family loss, homelessness, incarceration, and recovery before rebuilding toward sobriety, work, and family connection.

Her story connects the charts to the stakes: a person can move from loss and instability toward recovery when support is reachable and trusted.
