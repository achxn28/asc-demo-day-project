Every major claim needs a source.

Bridge LA uses official public datasets for counts, maps, housing context, and source notes. Each source below links back to the original publisher.

Resource-access methodology

The resource-access view compares mapped service records with homelessness and housing-pressure indicators. It is a student-project interpretation, not an official government designation.

Source trail

Where the numbers come from

The Homeless Count values come from LAHSA, housing-pressure context comes from Census/LAHD sources, service locations come from LA County/211 records, and story links come from local service organizations.
