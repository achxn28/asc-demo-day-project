Sources & checks

Every major claim needs a source.

Bridge LA uses official public datasets where factual values appear. If a metric cannot be verified, the interface omits it or labels the limitation.

Resource-access methodology

Bridge LA’s access indicator is a derived prototype concept: compare mapped service counts against need indicators such as LAHSA homelessness geography and ACS poverty rates. It is not an official LA government designation, and the project must not fill missing tract data with invented values.
