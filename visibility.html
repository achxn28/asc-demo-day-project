Visibility dashboard

Numbers calculated from traceable datasets.

This dashboard uses LAHSA 2026 totals and real mapped service records. It avoids current-availability or occupancy claims that are not publicly verifiable.

Data honesty: Dashboard numbers are source-backed counts or clearly labeled derived indicators, not live availability.

Service categories represented

Counts are calculated from the local copy of official LA County/211 service records included in this project.

Source: County of Los Angeles Homeless Shelters and Services feature service

Bridge LA resource-access indicator

For this static prototype, Bridge LA labels areas with fewer mapped resources near high-need homelessness/poverty geographies as a derived indicator only. It is not an official government designation, and poverty tract values are not substituted if the Census API cannot load.
