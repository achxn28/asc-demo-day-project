Public indicators

What the public data can show right now.

This page brings together LAHSA 2026 totals, mapped service records, and public dashboard update schedules so the resource landscape is easier to compare.

Availability note: Provider records show where services exist. For open beds, appointments, or wait times, call the provider or 211 LA.

Service categories represented

Where records cluster

Los Angeles has the largest cluster of mapped records, followed by Long Beach, Lancaster, Pasadena, and Hollywood. The pattern reflects where the public 211/County service layer has visible entries.

How fresh is each public dashboard?

Homeless Count data is annual, system KPIs are quarterly, interim-housing dashboards are monthly, and Pathway Home reporting is delayed by several days.

Public capacity tools

LAHSA has announced provider-network access to real-time interim-housing bed availability. Public users can also review LAHSA dashboards, seasonal 211 winter shelter guidance, and the LA Controller’s shelter bed demo map.

Questions the dashboard raises

Where are services clustered?

Most visible records cluster in Los Angeles, with smaller groups in Long Beach, Lancaster, Pasadena, Hollywood, and Santa Monica.

Which category dominates?

Shelter records make up most of the mapped entries, while healthcare entries are much less common in this dataset.

Who needs a call?

Anyone seeking a bed, appointment, or same-day help still needs provider contact information because public records are not live availability.
